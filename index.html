The experience

Drive through the tunnel.

Eurotunnel Roblox focuses on vehicle-based roleplay around border control, roads and the wider tunnel environment.

Civilian vehicles

Drive civilian cars through checkpoints and highways while roleplaying as part of the travelling public.

Police vehicles add another roleplay option for players around the roads, checkpoints and border environment.
